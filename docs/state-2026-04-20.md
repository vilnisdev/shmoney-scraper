## Targeting nuance (see #32)

Of the 605 owner+phone rows, some have real websites and some don't.
Highest-likelihood / highest-value leads are the **no-website** rows —
they need what we sell and the pitch is shortest. The current
qualification filter only drops *strong* real sites (score≥4); weak
real sites still land in the Sheet and outrank no-website rows.

Issue #32 tracks adding a priority-ordering pass (no-website-first,
then by score ascending) so the operator calls the ripest leads first.
